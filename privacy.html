Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Plain English version: We only collect your email if you subscribe. We use it to send you workouts. We never sell it. You can unsubscribe anytime with one click.

Who we are

HomeWOD (homewod.fit) is a free AI workout generator. We generate personalised home gym workouts using Claude AI. HomeWOD is an independent project.

What we collect

We only collect data when you actively provide it:

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to daily WOD emails or weekly program emails.
  • Workout preferences — equipment, fitness level, and training mode, saved alongside your email to personalise the workouts we send you.

We do not collect your name, location, payment details, or any other personal information. We do not use cookies for tracking. Workout history is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent to our servers.

How we use it

Your email and preferences are used for one purpose only: generating and sending you personalised workouts.

  • Daily WOD subscribers — we generate a workout tailored to your equipment and level each morning and send it to your inbox.
  • Weekly program subscribers — we generate a full training week based on your goals each Monday and send it to your inbox.

We do not send marketing emails, promotional content, or third-party offers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use HomeWOD (e.g. how many WODs are generated, which features are used). This data is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual users from it. You can opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Affiliate links

HomeWOD participates in the Amazon Associates programme. If you click an equipment link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you. This is disclosed wherever affiliate links appear.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate HomeWOD:

  • Resend — email delivery (resend.com)
  • Upstash — database for storing subscriber preferences (upstash.com)
  • Anthropic — AI model that generates your workouts (anthropic.com). Your workout preferences are sent to Anthropic's API to generate workouts. No personal data beyond preferences is shared.
  • Vercel — hosting (vercel.com)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any other parties.

How to unsubscribe

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Clicking it immediately removes you from that list — no confirmation steps, no re-engagement emails. Your data is deleted from our database within seconds.

Data retention

We store your email and preferences for as long as you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your data is deleted immediately. We have no archive of unsubscribed users.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any privacy questions, email us at hello@homewod.fit.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not retroactively reduce your privacy rights without notifying you.
